Plumbing help sorted by what is going wrong

Most calls start with a symptom rather than a service name. Slow or backed-up drains lead to drain cleaning, hydro-jetting and the sewer pages for cleaning, camera inspection and repair. Water you cannot explain points to leak detection, slab leak repair or burst pipe repair.

Hot water trouble ranges from repair to replacement, installation and tankless units. Fixture problems sit with toilets, faucets and disposals. Bigger projects include commercial plumbing, repiping, plumbing installation and water softener and filtration.

Urgent work versus planned work

Water gushing, sewage rising and tank heaters leaking are urgent, and the 24/7 line at (949) 325-3122 handles them at any hour. A same-day slot is offered when one is open, and otherwise we book the next opening. Planned jobs such as new fixtures or a softener are scheduled around your day.

In both cases a licensed and insured plumber diagnoses first and gives a clear price before work starts. You decide whether to go ahead, and nobody pressures you on the spot. Ask any question you like before saying yes, because that is part of the visit.

Choosing the right service when you are not sure

If you cannot tell whether you have a fixture clog or a main line problem, describe what is slow and when it started. One fixture means a local clog, while several fixtures together suggest the main. The plumber confirms on site, sometimes with a camera. A tank heater that is cold may have a bad element, a dead thermostat or a pilot that will not stay lit, and each has a different fix.

You do not need to know the technical name for your problem. Call, explain what you see and hear, and we match the visit to it. The home page also has a quick table on what can wait until morning and what cannot.
