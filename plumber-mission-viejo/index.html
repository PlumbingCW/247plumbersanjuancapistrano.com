When the rain starts, check these before you call

Neighboring cities in this part of Orange County see roughly 14 inches of rain in a typical year, so when a storm does arrive, flooded yards and overloaded drains catch people off guard. If you need an emergency plumber in Mission Viejo during a storm, call (949) 325-3122. The 24/7 line is answered through the worst of the weather.

Mission Viejo is a planned community built with roads in valleys and homes on hills. Water runs downhill, and so can the problem: a backup in a low-lying home may start from a line shared with houses above it. If your drains slow only when it rains, say so on the call.

Hard water through SMWD's service area

Mission Viejo sits inside the Santa Margarita Water District service area. The district's 2026 report for neighboring San Juan Capistrano put average hardness at 12 grains per gallon, and a range of 5.2 to 17 across that system. We cannot say what your tap measures, so test it or read the report your own address is covered by.

Why it matters: hard water leaves scale on water heater elements and tank bottoms, and on the heat exchanger of a tankless unit. Run a little hot water into a bucket and look for grit or white flakes, because that is scale breaking loose. Scale insulates the heating surface, so the heater works harder and wears out sooner. A water softener or filtration system addresses the cause, while a flush and new anode address the symptom.

Hillside homes, water heaters and the repair choice

Tank heaters last about ten years, and Mission Viejo's planned-community homes, much of it complete by 1980, include many heaters that have been replaced at least once. A heater that rattles or runs out of hot water fast is carrying scale. If you are choosing between repair and replacement, our water heater repair page lists what we check first.

If the tank is leaking, repair is off the table and the question becomes tank or tankless. Tankless units save space but need descaling on a schedule, which matters more where hardness runs high.
