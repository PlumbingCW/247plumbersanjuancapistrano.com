Plumbing Service Area Around San Juan Capistrano, CA

Our crews are dispatched from San Juan Capistrano and cover five neighboring towns. Find yours below, or call (949) 325-3122 to confirm your address.

Call (949) 325-3122

The line is answered at every hour.

Home base on Del Obispo Street

Dispatch is based at 31888 Del Obispo Street in San Juan Capistrano, ZIP 92675, and the city's other ZIP code, 92693, is covered as well. Interstate 5 runs through the city, with interchanges at Camino Capistrano, Ortega Highway and Junipero Serra Road, so crews have several routes to most addresses. That lets one base serve a stretch of south Orange County.

A crew heading to Dana Point can follow Del Obispo Street, while a crew heading north or south uses the freeway. Which route is faster on a given night depends on traffic, which is why we do not quote arrival times.

Neighboring towns we dispatch to

Dana Point lies southwest of us, along the coast, and salt air there is a corrosion factor for exposed fittings. San Clemente is to the southeast, and Laguna Niguel is to the northwest, with Crown Valley Parkway as its main road.

North of San Juan Capistrano are Mission Viejo, a planned community built largely by 1980, and Rancho Santa Margarita, which is generally newer. Each town page lists its ZIP codes and the services we see most often there. Reading the page for your town is a good way to see what typically goes wrong in your kind of house before you call.

Who bills you for water, and who fixes the pipe

The Santa Margarita Water District serves San Juan Capistrano, Mission Viejo, Rancho Santa Margarita and Talega, which is part of San Clemente. Laguna Niguel gets its water from the Moulton Niguel Water District. For other addresses, check your bill to see which agency sends it.

The practical point is where the utility's responsibility ends. A district looks after the main line and the meter. The pipes on your side of the meter, plus the fixtures and heater, belong to the property. If your bill suddenly jumps or the meter spins with everything off, call (949) 325-3122 and we will help you sort out which side of the meter the problem sits on.

If your address is on the edge of the map

Some customers call from a property they manage, a rental or a small shop rather than a home. The same line handles those jobs. Give the dispatcher the street address of the building and a contact who can let the plumber in, and the visit can be planned around your schedule.

Roads and boundaries do not always match what people call their neighborhood. If your home is between towns or in a ZIP code we have not listed, call (949) 325-3122 and tell us the street address. We would rather confirm coverage on the phone than guess here.

Whatever your town, the same rules apply: a licensed and insured plumber, a diagnosis on site, and a clear price before work starts. See the plumbing services page for the types of jobs we handle.
