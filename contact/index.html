Call the 24/7 line

Phone is the only way we take plumbing requests. Call (949) 325-3122 at any hour, including nights, weekends and holidays. We book the next available appointment, offer a same-day slot if one is open, and dispatch a licensed and insured plumber to your address.

We do not use web forms, chat windows or text threads for plumbing problems. A pipe that is leaking needs a conversation, not a ticket, and a call lets us ask the follow-up questions that matter.

What to have ready when you call

A few details save time on the phone and help send the right plumber with the right parts. None of it needs to be perfect. If you do not know something, say so.

  • The full street address and ZIP code, and any gate code or unit number.
  • What is happening: leak, clog, no hot water, smell, noise or something else.
  • Whether water is still running, and whether you have reached the main shutoff.
  • Where in the building it is happening, such as the kitchen, garage or yard.
  • How old the fixture or heater is, if you know.
  • A good number to reach you, and whether you own, rent or manage the property.

Property managers can add the tenant contact and any access instructions. If a tenant will be home, say so, because it saves a missed visit.

Emergency or non-urgent?

Call right away if water is flooding a floor, a pipe has split, sewage is coming up through a drain, or a tank heater is leaking heavily. In those cases, shut off the main valve first if you can reach it, then call. If you smell gas, leave the building and call your gas utility or 911 before you call us.

Slow drains, dripping faucets, a toilet that runs, a disposal that hums and similar problems are non-urgent. You can still call at night and we will book the next available daytime appointment, so you are not up worrying. Our plumbing services page describes what each type of job involves.

Our address and email

You can find the office at 31888 Del Obispo Street, San Juan Capistrano, CA 92675. Plumbers spend their days in the field, so please call before you stop by.

For non-plumbing questions about this website or the business, the email address is contact@247plumbersanjuancapistrano.com. Email is not monitored for emergencies, and we cannot diagnose or book a plumber by email, so phone us for anything involving water, drains or heat.

Where the crews come from
