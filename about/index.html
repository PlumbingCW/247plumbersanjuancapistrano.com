What this business does

San Juan Capistrano 24/7 Plumbing dispatches licensed and insured plumbers to homes and businesses in San Juan Capistrano and the nearby towns of Dana Point, San Clemente, Laguna Niguel, Mission Viejo and Rancho Santa Margarita. Our office is at 31888 Del Obispo Street, and our phone line is answered around the clock at (949) 325-3122.

When you call, we listen to the problem, take your address and book the next available appointment. A same-day slot is offered if one is open. A plumber then diagnoses the work in person and gives you a clear price before work starts.

We keep this page short on bragging and long on facts, because a plumbing call often happens when you are stressed. You deserve to know who you are dealing with before a stranger comes into your house. Plumbing work in California follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), and a legitimate contractor can explain how a job meets it.

What we do not claim

You will not find named staff, a founding year, job counts, review scores or license numbers on this site. We would rather leave those out than print something that cannot be checked from this page. Where a trust claim matters, we point you to a public source you can check yourself.

We also do not promise arrival times. Traffic on I-5, the job ahead of yours and the nature of your problem all change the schedule. What we can do is tell you honestly what the next step is when you call.

Hard water, old adobe and why a local dispatcher matters

Water service in town changed hands on November 15, 2021, when the Santa Margarita Water District took it over. The district's 2026 report for reporting year 2025 puts average hardness at 210 mg/L, about 12 grains per gallon, with a range of 89 to 290 mg/L across the system. That is hard water, and it leaves scale on heater elements and tank bottoms.

For a homeowner this means a heater that works harder each year and fixtures that spot. When we discuss a repair or a replacement, scale is one of the first things on the list, and a plumber who works here every week sees it more often than a visitor would.

Los Rios Street is another local reality. Its historic district is called the oldest residential neighborhood in California, and work in very old buildings takes patience and the right questions. If your home is anywhere near that age, say so when you call, and the plumber will plan around it.

How to verify any plumber's license

California licenses plumbing contractors through the Contractors State License Board, and its public lookup at cslb.ca.gov lets you search by license number or business name. Check that the license is current and that the classification fits plumbing work, which is the C-36 classification. Look at any actions on file as well.

Do this for us and for anyone else you consider. It takes a few minutes, and it is the best protection a homeowner has. If a plumber will not give you a license number when asked, treat that as an answer.

What to have ready when the phone is answered

  • The street address and the nearest cross street, so the dispatcher can plan the route.
  • What you see and hear, such as water on a ceiling, a gurgling drain or a cold shower.
  • Whether you have already shut off the main water valve, and where it is.
  • A gate code, a unit number or a property manager's name if the building is not a single-family home.
  • Whether anyone will be home to open the door.

None of this is a test. If you do not know an answer, say so and we will work out the next step together over the phone.

Questions worth asking any plumber before work starts

  • Can I see your license number and proof of insurance?
  • What exactly is the problem, and how did you confirm it?
  • What is the full price for this job, and what could change it?
  • Is a permit needed, and who handles it?
  • What happens to the old parts, and can I see them?
  • What is covered if something fails after the repair?

A good plumber answers these without irritation. The answers also help you compare options. If you want a deeper look at what we handle, the plumbing services page lists every category.
