What sea air does to fittings near the harbor

Dana Point Harbor, Doheny State Beach and Strands Beach put a lot of homes within reach of salt air. Salt is a general corrosion factor for anything metal left exposed, such as hose bibs, shutoff valves, exterior hangers and water heater connections in an open garage. The practical result is that small weeps at fittings turn into real leaks sooner than they would inland.

So look at your exposed fittings twice a year. Green crust or flaking on a valve is a repair you can schedule before it becomes an emergency call. If a fitting is already dripping, a leak detection visit finds out whether the drip is the whole problem or only the part you can see.
