San Juan Hills: call to talk through your plumbing problem.

Many small communities around one golf course

San Juan Hills sits on the east side of San Juan Capistrano. One real estate source describes it as a series of private communities around the San Juan Hills Golf Club, with subdivisions that include Mesa Vista, Loma Vista, San Juan Hills West and San Juan Hills East. The same source puts the median build year near 1978 and counts about 1,777 homes.

Those numbers come from a listing site, not from the city, so treat them as a guide. Boundaries are not documented, and we will not draw them for you. Call (949) 325-3122 with your street and your community name, and we will ask the rest.

Who fixes what: owner or association

In townhomes and condos the first plumbing question is not what broke. It is who owns the part that broke. In one community in the area, each block is reported to have its own association and its own pool and spa, and owners are reported to be responsible for their own roofs and garage doors. Rules differ block by block.

  • Read your governing documents to see where association pipes end and owner pipes begin.
  • Contact your management company before work when a shared wall or line is involved.
  • Keep photos and the date if water crosses a wall into a neighbor's unit.
  • Ask whether your association wants notice or names a preferred contractor.

We fix the plumbing. We cannot tell you what your association's documents say, and we will not guess.

Homes from the late 1970s and what to ask

One source reports that San Juan Hills East was built in 1978 and 1979, and the neighborhood median build year is listed near 1978. A house that age may have had parts replaced, or may not. The date alone tells us nothing about your pipes.

So we ask practical questions. Has the water heater been replaced? Have any pipes been repaired or swapped? Is there a record of past leaks? A sewer camera run and a pressure test answer what paperwork cannot.

If water is coming through a ceiling or wall

Do these steps and then call.

Call (949) 325-3122 now
  1. Close the shutoff that serves your unit. If you cannot find it, say so when you call.
  2. Clear belongings away from the dripping area.
  3. Alert the neighbor above or beside you if you can do it safely, since the source may be theirs.
  4. Write down the time you first noticed the water and take photos.
  5. Call (949) 325-3122 with your community name, street number and the wall or ceiling affected.

Water heaters, hard water and older bathrooms

The city's water is hard, and scale shortens the life of heating parts in any home. A water heater repair may buy time, but a failing tank is a replacement. If you are comparing options, see our page on tankless water heaters, and note that scale affects them too.

Nearby neighborhoods ask different questions. For a larger gated community, see Marbella.
