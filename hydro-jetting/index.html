What pressurized water does that a blade cannot

Hydro jetting in San Juan Capistrano, CA sends pressurized water through a nozzle that scours the whole pipe wall instead of poking one channel through the middle. A line packed with grease or roots is an emergency the moment it backs up into a tub or floor drain, and the 24/7 line at (949) 325-3122 takes those calls at any hour. Stop using water until the plumber says the line is open.

The nozzle pulls itself forward on the thrust of its own jets, so the hose trails behind with little strain. Water cuts the buildup loose and carries it downstream. The result is a pipe that looks cleaned, not just unblocked.

Tree roots working into the joints

Roots go looking for water, and a small crack or loose joint in a sewer line is a steady supply. Once inside, they thicken into a mat that snags paper and waste. A main that slows a little more each season often traces back to a root mass.

A jet with a root-cutting nozzle shreds the mat and washes the pieces out. It does not remove the root outside the pipe, so regrowth is possible. A camera visit afterward, described under sewer inspection, shows whether a joint needs real repair.

Grease that sets like candle wax

Restaurants and home kitchens both leave fat in the line. Fat cools, hardens and traps paper, wipes and scraps until the pipe narrows to a slit. Cable machines punch a hole through it and leave the rest behind, which is why the same sink backs up again in a month.

Hot water jetting softens fat on contact and flushes it far downstream. A busy restaurant kitchen usually does better on a planned cleaning schedule than waiting for the next backup.

A line that returns to normal and then fails again is a coated pipe. The cure is to clean the walls, which is where jetting earns its keep. If a cable seized in the pipe, tell the dispatcher, since a snagged tool changes how the plumber starts.

When jetting is the wrong tool

Pressure is not gentle on a pipe that is already failing. A badly cracked clay pipe or a line with a belly can be damaged or leave debris behind. For that reason a camera look often comes first on older lines, and the plumber may choose a cable instead. Drain lines inside the house are usually cleaned by drain cleaning with a cable.
