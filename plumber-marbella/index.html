Marbella is a residential community in San Juan Capistrano built around the Marbella Country Club and its golf course. Local real estate sources describe it as gated, and city planning documents give access from Rancho Viejo Road. Marbella Plaza, a shopping center, carries the same name.

What gates and a homeowners association change

Gated entries and association rules affect a service visit more than most people expect. Check these before you call, so the visit goes smoothly.

  • Put our visit on the gate list or leave instructions for the guard so we are not stopped at the entry.
  • Learn what your association maintains, such as shared slopes or common-area lines, and what stays with you.
  • See whether your association asks for notice before contractor work or restricts work hours.
  • Find your main shutoff before you need it, because large houses often have more than one.

We do not know your association's rules, and they differ between communities. Your management company or governing documents are the right source.

Multiple water heaters and long hot-water runs

A big house often has more than one water heater or a long run from the heater to the far bathroom. A wait for hot water at the farthest tap is a design trait, not always a fault. A failing heater shows other signs: rust-colored water, popping noises, water around the base or a unit that no longer keeps up.

Hard water is the usual wear factor across the city, and scale builds on heating parts. When you replace a water heater, ask whether a softener or filter makes sense for the house.

Hidden leaks in a big house

A leak in a large house can run a long time before a stain appears. Two quick checks help. Look at the water meter with every fixture off, and watch for a spinning dial. Then check your bill for a jump with no change in how you use water.

If either check fails, book a leak detection visit before any wall is opened. If the floor feels warm in one spot, read about slab leak repair. Neighbors in nearby communities face the same questions, such as in the San Juan Hills area.
