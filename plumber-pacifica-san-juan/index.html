A newer community with its own set of plumbing questions

Pacifica San Juan is a master-planned, gated community of single-family homes on about 259 acres, just off Interstate 5. It was reported as sitting on a bluff overlooking San Juan Capistrano and Dana Point. Development stalled after 2008 and restarted in 2016, with builders including Taylor Morrison and Toll Brothers.

Neighborhoods inside it carry names such as The Cove, The Peninsula, Crystal Downs South and The Pearl, with lot sizes that differ a lot between them. We do not know the plumbing in any one house, but a newer home asks different questions from an old one. Call (949) 325-3122 and tell us what you notice.

Is it still under warranty? Ask this first

A newer home may still carry a builder or manufacturer warranty on some parts. Before paying for a repair, find out whether that is true for your house. A repair done by the wrong party can complicate a claim.

  • Check your closing documents for builder warranty terms and dates.
  • Find the manufacturer paperwork for your water heater, softener and fixtures.
  • Ask your association what it covers and what it does not.
  • Photograph the problem and keep dates before anyone touches it.

If a warranty applies, use it. If it has ended or does not cover the part, call us and we will take it from there.

Newer homes, newer equipment, and the same hard water

Newer homes often come with modern equipment, sometimes including tankless water heaters and water treatment. We cannot say what is in your house until we see it. What we can say is that the city's water is hard. The district's 2025 report gives an average near 12 grains per gallon, and the range runs up to 17.

Scale builds on heat exchangers and heating elements whatever the age of the house. If you have a tankless unit, ask about scale maintenance on our tankless water heater page. If you do not have a softener, read about water softeners and filtration.

Irrigation, yards and outdoor lines

Larger lots mean more irrigation. A stuck valve or a cracked line can waste water for weeks without a visible puddle. If the bill jumps, we check the meter, the irrigation valves and the hose bibs before we look anywhere else.

Outdoor shutoffs and backflow devices are worth finding now. Photograph them with your phone so the information is at hand when you need it.

Working with a gated community and near Dana Point

As in any gated community, leave gate instructions so a visit is not delayed. Ask your association whether it requires notice for contractor work or sets work hours. We follow the rules you give us.

The community sits near the Dana Point side of San Juan Capistrano. If you split time between the two towns, our page on plumbing in Dana Point may also help. For a larger established golf community, see Marbella.

Even in a new build, repairs and add-ons fall under the California Plumbing Code, which is built on the Uniform Plumbing Code. Visit the home page for San Juan Capistrano to see every service.
